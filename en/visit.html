Visitor Info

Dates, venue, tickets and official links — everything you need to plan the trip.

When does the exhibition run?expand_more
September 2–4, 2026 (Wed–Fri). Hours: Wed 10:00–17:00, Thu 10:00–17:00, Fri 10:00–16:00.
Where is it, and how do I get there?expand_more
Taipei Nangang Exhibition Center, Halls 1 & 2 (TaiNEX 1 & 2). Take the Taipei Metro to Taipei Nangang Exhibition Center Station (Bannan Blue Line / Wenhu Brown Line).
When are the international forums?expand_more
Forums run Monday Aug 31 through Friday Sep 4 — they begin before the exhibition floor opens, so plan forum days separately from your show-floor visit.
How do I register or collect a badge?expand_more
Register online via the official SEMICON Taiwan site, then collect your badge on-site. Check the official page for the latest pricing and any limited-time admission offers.

Who organizes the show?expand_more
SEMICON Taiwan is organized by SEMI, co-organized by TSIA (Taiwan Semiconductor Industry Association), with guidance from Taiwan's Ministry of Economic Affairs (MOEA).
